1. Executive Summary

In 2026 the local-SLM landscape consolidated around 4-bit quantization (Q4_K_M / GGUF) as the practical default for consumer hardware. The key shift is not raw parameter count but efficiency per gigabyte of VRAM:

  • Phi-4 (14B) — strongest reasoning-per-VRAM in the 12–16 GB class.
  • Llama 3.3 70B — the "big local" reference, but needs 40–48 GB VRAM at Q4.
  • DeepSeek R1 Distill — dominates chain-of-thought reasoning at small sizes, with longer outputs.
  • Qwen3 / Gemma 3–4 — main challengers in the sub-8B edge tier.

3. Key 2026 Trends

  1. Q4 is the new default — ~3% quality loss vs. 4× VRAM savings.
  2. Reasoning models changed the metric — "useful tokens/sec" (accounting for CoT overhead) now matters more than raw tokens/sec.
  3. The 12 GB tier is the sweet spot — Phi-4 14B and DeepSeek-14B both land there.
  4. 70B is workstation-only — still not consumer-viable at usable quality.
  5. Edge models (3–4B) matured — Qwen3-4B and Gemma 3-4B now handle real assistant tasks.
  6. Gemma 4 (Apache 2.0) — Google's license switch removed a major adoption barrier.
